# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Romanian small business owners, entrepreneurs, and professionals (doctors, lawyers, restaurants, agencies) who need a professional website but lack technical skills or time to build one. They're busy running their business and want someone competent to handle the website entirely. They arrive at webform.ro looking for a fast, hassle-free path to a professional online presence.

## Product Purpose

WebForm is a subscription-based website building and management service. Clients submit a structured "Blueprint" form describing their needs, choose from 100+ premium templates, and WebForm's team builds, hosts, and manages their website end-to-end. No meetings, no DIY builders, no technical knowledge required. A professional site delivered in 7 days with unlimited ongoing management. Success looks like: a business owner submits the form, picks a template, and has a polished live website within a week — then never worries about it again.

## Brand Personality

Professional, Confident, Effortless.

The voice is direct and assured — WebForm knows what it's doing and communicates that without arrogance. The tone is Romanian-first, using natural conversational Romanian (not corporate-speak). The feeling should be: "these people are competent and will take care of everything." Effortlessness is the core emotional promise — the entire experience should feel like handing off a task to someone you trust.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS**: Not another Stripe/Linear clone with the same radial gradients, glass cards, and purple-to-blue color stories. The dark-mode-with-gradient-orbs pattern is exactly what the current site looks like and what we're moving away from.
- **Cheap website builders**: Must not look like Wix, Squarespace, or WordPress theme previews. WebForm is a premium managed service, not a DIY tool. No builder-style dashboards, no drag-and-drop metaphors.
- **Over-designed agencies**: Not an award-hunting portfolio site that's all parallax and confusion. Navigation and information hierarchy must be immediately clear. Beautiful but usable, not beautiful but lost.

## Design Principles

1. **Effortless clarity** — Every screen should make the next action obvious. If a visitor has to think about where to click, the design failed.
2. **Show the work** — The template gallery and blueprint form ARE the product demo. Let the quality of the templates sell the service; don't hide them behind marketing fluff.
3. **Earned confidence** — Premium feeling comes from restraint and precision (typography, spacing, rhythm), not from decoration (gradients, glass, glow effects).
4. **Respect the visitor's time** — Romanian business owners are busy. Dense, scannable content over sprawling storytelling. Get to the point.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific WCAG compliance target. Maintain sensible contrast ratios, readable font sizes, and functional keyboard navigation. Romanian diacritical characters (ă, â, î, ș, ț) must render correctly across all fonts used.
